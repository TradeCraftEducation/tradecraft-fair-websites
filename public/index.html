The problem

Right now, your show's information is scattered

Most fair and livestock show websites weren't built — they accumulated. The result is familiar to any board member:

  • Dates that got updated on Facebook but never made it to the website
  • Rulebooks and entry forms buried in old PDFs nobody can find
  • One volunteer who knows how to edit the site — and is unreachable during show week
  • Exhibitors and buyers texting board members for information that should be online
  • Frantic, last-minute updates the week the show is actually happening

Send us the changes. We keep the website current. That's what Website Care is for.
