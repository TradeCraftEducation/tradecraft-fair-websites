The problem

Every fair board runs into the same thing

Dates live in three different places. The rulebook PDF on Facebook isn't the current one. One volunteer knows the login, and they're not always answering texts during show week.

  • Show dates and deadlines scattered across Facebook, old PDFs, and word of mouth
  • A website only one board member knows how to update
  • Exhibitors and buyers calling or texting the board for basic answers
  • Frantic last-minute updates during the one week traffic actually matters

Our answer is simple: send us the changes. We keep the website current — including during show week.
